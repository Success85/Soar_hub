# Applicant & reviewer guide

## Applicants
1. **Create an account** and confirm your email (check spam). Sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity.
2. **Set up your profile** step by step — save and come back any time. Sensitive questions are optional; we
   explain why each question matters.
3. **Explore scholarships**: search and filter; every listing shows its official source and when it was verified.
   Save the ones you like.
4. **Check your eligibility**: we ask only what we don't already know, most decisive first. You'll see each
   criterion as *meets*, *does not meet* or *undetermined*, with what you can do. Results are guidance, not a
   guarantee. Update your profile any time — results recalculate automatically and history is kept.
5. **Start an application plan**: you get a milestone schedule worked backward from the deadline (with a safety
   buffer). Mark tasks complete, add notes, adjust dates, or reset to the recommended plan. List, timeline and
   calendar views; reminders 7 days, 1 day and on the day (SMS optional).
6. **Documents**: upload PDF, Word, JPEG or PNG (up to the limit shown). Each upload is a new version — nothing
   is overwritten. Request a review to get comments linked to the exact passage.
7. **Essays & answer bank**: draft in your own words with a live word count and full version history; compare
   versions; submit for review. AI may suggest improvements, but a human checks every suggestion first and AI never
   rewrites your essay. Store your best stories in the answer bank and find them again for new prompts.
8. **Referees**: send a secure, single-use upload link (no account needed, valid 14 days). Track opened/submitted,
   send a fresh link, or revoke it.
9. **Submit** on the official university portal, then mark your application submitted (allowed once all required
   documents and tasks are done). Record shortlisting and outcomes when you hear back.
10. **Settings & privacy**: notification preferences, password, download your data, or request deletion.

## Reviewers / coaches
- **My assignments** lists applicants assigned to you; you can't see anyone else's records or sensitive details.
- **Document reviews**: open a document, *Start review*, select text (or paste an exact passage) to add an
  anchored comment, then record a decision (approve / return for revision / reject) with a summary. Comments
  stay attached to that version.
- **Essay reviews**: comment on passages, review the AI critique (edit any field, approve to publish, or reject
  with a reason), then complete the review (request revision or mark ready).
- Ask questions and point to evidence — don't rewrite applicants' words.
